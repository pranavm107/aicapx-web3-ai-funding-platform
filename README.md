# AICapX
### Bridging AI Innovation and Decentralized Investment

> A blockchain-powered RWA investment platform connecting AI startups with investors through fractional tokenization and decentralized finance.

---

## About the Project

**AICapX** is a team-based hackathon project focused on simplifying fundraising for AI startups through Real-World Asset (RWA) tokenization.

Many early-stage AI startups require significant capital to access high-performance GPU infrastructure, train advanced AI models, acquire datasets, and scale their products. Traditional fundraising often involves lengthy procedures, intermediaries, and limited investment accessibility.

AICapX aims to address these challenges by connecting startups and investors through a blockchain-powered investment ecosystem built on **BNB Smart Chain**.

Startups can submit their projects, undergo verification, and raise funds by offering fractional digital investment tokens. Investors can explore opportunities, purchase tokens, and track their holdings transparently on the blockchain.

## Problem Statement

AI startups often face significant financial barriers when developing and deploying advanced AI solutions.

Traditional fundraising processes can be:
- Time-consuming and complicated.
- Dependent on multiple financial intermediaries.
- Difficult for smaller investors to access.
- Limited in investment transparency.

## Our Solution

AICapX introduces a decentralized investment marketplace that enables:

- Startup project submission and verification.
- Administrative review and approval.
- Fractional investment tokenization using ERC-1155.
- Blockchain-based ownership tracking.
- Transparent investment transactions.
- Revenue-sharing mechanisms based on defined contractual rights.

## Key Features

- **Startup Onboarding:** Submit company details, project information, certificates, and supporting documents.
- **Startup Verification:** Administrative review before marketplace approval.
- **Fractional Tokenization:** Represent investment rights through ERC-1155 tokens.
- **Investor Marketplace:** Discover and evaluate AI startup investment opportunities.
- **Blockchain Transparency:** Verify token minting and transfers on BNB Smart Chain.
- **Investment Dashboard:** Track holdings and investment activity.
- **Revenue Distribution:** Support transparent distribution according to predefined investment agreements.

## Blockchain Implementation

AICapX uses BNB Smart Chain to provide transparent and verifiable investment token operations.

The platform uses ERC-1155 to represent fractional investment interests associated with individual startup projects.

Approved projects can trigger token minting through the administrative workflow. Blockchain transactions can be independently verified using BscScan.

## Project Workflow

1. Startup registers and submits a project.
2. Supporting company and project documents are uploaded.
3. Admin reviews and verifies the submission.
4. Approved projects undergo fractional token minting.
5. Projects become available in the investment marketplace.
6. Investors purchase available investment tokens.
7. Ownership and eligible distributions are tracked through the platform.
